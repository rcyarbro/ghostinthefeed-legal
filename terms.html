1. About the application

Ghost in the Feed Publisher (the "App") is a personal, self-hosted publishing tool based on the open-source project Postiz. It is used solely by the owner of the TikTok account @ghostinthefeed79 (the "Owner") to upload and schedule the Owner's own original videos to that TikTok account.

2. Single user only

The App is not offered to the public. There is no sign-up, and no one other than the Owner uses it or can create an account in it. It is run only on the Owner's own computer.

3. TikTok integration

The App connects to TikTok through TikTok Login Kit (scope user.info.basic) and the TikTok Content Posting API (scopes video.upload and video.publish). These permissions are used only to identify the Owner's TikTok account and to upload and publish the Owner's own videos to it.

4. Content

All content published through the App is the Owner's own original work. The Owner is responsible for that content and for ensuring it complies with TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

5. Revoking access

The Owner can revoke the App's access to the TikTok account at any time through TikTok's settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services / Manage app permissions).
