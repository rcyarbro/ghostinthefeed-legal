Privacy Policy

Ghost in the Feed Publisher · Effective date: October 9, 2026

1. Overview

Ghost in the Feed Publisher (the "App") is a personal, self-hosted publishing tool based on the open-source project Postiz. It is used solely by the owner of the TikTok account @ghostinthefeed79 (the "Owner") to upload and schedule the Owner's own original videos.

2. Single user; no data from other users

The App has exactly one user: the Owner. It does not collect, request, or process any personal data from any other person or TikTok user.

3. Information the App stores

When the Owner connects a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit (user.info.basic) and the Content Posting API (video.upload, video.publish), the App stores only:

  • the TikTok OAuth access and refresh tokens issued for the Owner's account; and
  • basic profile information for that account: display name and avatar.

This information is stored only on the Owner's own computer and is used only to upload and publish the Owner's videos to the Owner's TikTok account.

4. No selling or sharing

No data is sold, rented, shared with, or disclosed to any third party. The App contains no advertising or analytics. Data is sent only to TikTok, as required to publish the Owner's videos.

5. Retention and deletion

Stored tokens and profile information remain on the Owner's computer only while the TikTok connection is active. Disconnecting the account in the App or uninstalling it removes them.

6. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time through TikTok's settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services / Manage app permissions). Once revoked, the stored tokens no longer work.

7. Compliance with TikTok terms

The App uses TikTok's APIs in accordance with TikTok's Developer Terms, and all content published through it complies with TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version will always be published at this address with its effective date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the GitHub repository's issues page.
